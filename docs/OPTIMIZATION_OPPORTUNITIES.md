# Optimization Opportunities

Time differences emerge from engineering quality: parallelism, power stability, routing, preparation, local production, and reduced downtime.

## 01 — Early Survival and Workshop

- Experienced players consolidate mining trips and build only durable workshop infrastructure.

Reuse: The workshop, furnaces, and Iron Chests become the intake area for the electrical factory.

## 02 — Electrification

- Keep machines powered, batch compatible work, and avoid needless voltage conversion.

Reuse: LV generation, storage, cabling, and processors remain feeder lines for every later factory.

## 03 — Automated Industry

- Start the Quarry early, feed processing directly, parallelize bottleneck machines, and buffer outputs.

Reuse: The Quarry, routes, processors, and autocrafter become the raw-material backbone of heavy industry.

## 04 — Heavy Industry

- Run steel and fuel continuously, reuse power/logistics trunks, and move bulk goods without inventories carried by hand.

Reuse: Existing ore processing, storage, Quarry output, and routing feed every heavy-industry line.

## 05 — Programmable Manufacturing

- Maintain buffers, detect shortages before stalls, monitor power, and parallelize slow physical processes.

Reuse: The physical factory remains intact; ComputerCraft adds supervision and scheduling rather than replacing machines.

## 06 — Nuclear Age

- Efficient layouts improve fuel use and output; automation reduces downtime and accelerates launch manufacturing.

Reuse: Heavy-industry materials, programmable monitoring, and existing power distribution become the space-program utility grid.

## 07 — Orbital Age

- Pre-manufacture station systems, pack launches carefully, and reuse orbital power and docking infrastructure.

Reuse: Nuclear manufacturing builds launch hardware; the orbital station remains a research, power, cargo, and shipyard hub.

## 08 — Orbital Research

- Right-size eclipse storage, keep experiments powered, transmit data automatically, and use orbital energy productively.

Reuse: The same station, solar array, docking, and Research Station support Moon logistics and later Mars traffic.

## 09 — Lunar Settlement

- Bring only startup essentials, establish local production early, and avoid importing every component from Earth.

Reuse: Orbital power, cargo handling, research equipment, and Earth factories remain part of the lunar supply network.

## 10 — Lunar Research

- Share power and logistics across experiments, keep lunar industry productive during research, and schedule around darkness.

Reuse: The lunar base, automated mine, station platform, and orbital link become Quantum manufacturing infrastructure.

## 11 — Quantum Technology

- Prepare high-tier power early, parallelize advanced materials, manufacture on the Moon, and maintain component buffers.

Reuse: Nuclear power, lunar industry, orbital logistics, and programmable factories are upgraded rather than discarded.

## 12 — Mars Settlement

- Pre-build a compact startup package, establish robust power and local production, and avoid repeated Earth–Mars trips.

Reuse: Earth, orbital, and lunar factories remain suppliers while Mars rapidly becomes locally productive.

## 13 — Martian Autonomy Research

- Mature programmable factories finish quickly; manual imports and intervention create the intended large pacing spread.

Reuse: The Martian base, robotic cell, ComputerCraft programs, and existing interplanetary logistics become the AI proving ground.

## 14 — Lite Matter Engineering

- Reuse prepared interplanetary samples, stable power, and automated material lines; avoid one-off rebuilding.

Reuse: The real Analyzer, Mars Sample, power grid, and material factories become inputs to AI-era matter research.

## 15 — AI Age

- A mature pre-AI factory turns AI entry into coordination rather than a new raw-material grind.

Reuse: Every prior production site and transport route becomes a managed node in the digital industrial network.

## 16 — Continuously Expanding Post-AI Civilization

- Optimize energy scale, throughput, resilience, network architecture, colony independence, and research priorities.

Reuse: The endgame scales and connects all previous infrastructure; no post-AI milestone is a prerequisite for AI entry.
