# Progression

Industrial Civilization uses a complete 16-chapter capability campaign rather
than a short demonstration route. Better Questing provides the detailed story,
instructions, and automatic evidence tasks; the single Industrial Civilization
advancement page mirrors the achievable order.

1. Survival Workshop — shelter, storage, manual production, and a secure workshop.
2. Electrification — IC2 generation, storage, cables, processing, and electric tools.
3. Automated Industry — ore handling, transport, quarrying, chunk loading, monitoring, and early autocrafting.
4. Heavy Industry — steel, fuel, freight, advanced IC2, armament, factions, containment, and industrial capacity.
5. Programmable Manufacturing — Electric Fabricator, Programmable Assembler, ComputerCraft control, queues, and multi-step production.
6. Nuclear Age — a monitored IC2 reactor, sustained output, telemetry, SCRAM control, and containment.
7. Orbital Age — a real Tier 1 launch, active-detector habitat, power, communications, research, experiments, and a sustained functional station.
8. Orbital Research — environment-tagged data, solar experiments, tracking power, and the Orbital Research Archive.
9. Lunar Settlement — gated Moon travel, habitat, mining, manufacturing, cargo, and a sustained functional base.
10. Lunar Research — lunar science, darkness endurance, precision manufacturing, and the Lunar Engineering Archive.
11. Quantum Technology — extreme-voltage infrastructure, robotic manufacturing, lunar quantum components, and Nano/Quantum equipment.
12. Mars Settlement — authorization, Tier 2 travel, habitat, Desh mining, manufacturing, cargo, and a sustained Martian base.
13. Martian Autonomy — unattended response, production, research, and the Martian Autonomy Archive.
14. Lite Matter Engineering — molecular analysis, origin-aware comparison, durable pattern records, and recovery without early replication.
15. AI Age — technical-memory audit, Artificial Industrial Intelligence Core synthesis, Technical Phase Pearl, and AI-authorized AE2 entry.
16. Post-AI Civilization — cross-planet logistics, UU-Matter, controlled replication, fusion, antimatter, megastructures, colonies, and civilization-scale AI.

The declared main-campaign completion milestone is
`continuous_civilization`. It does not end the world or disable continued play.
The full numbered campaign passed live acceptance in `Test Bed 1`, including
save/reload persistence. The seven independent side paths remain optional and
do not block campaign completion.

Factory capability—not arbitrary component inflation—is the pacing lever. The
intended passive chest ceiling is Iron Chest. Gold, Diamond, Crystal, and
Obsidian Chest recipes are removed and hidden; powered machines, warehouses,
pipes, and logistics provide larger-scale storage.

Canonical prerequisites and milestone data live in `progression/`. Generated
summaries are in `docs/PROGRESSION_OVERVIEW.md` and `docs/CRITICAL_PATH.md`;
the exact live procedure and results are in `docs/MAIN_QUESTLINE_TEST_PLAN.md`
and `docs/MAIN_QUESTLINE_LIVE_TEST_LOG.md`.
