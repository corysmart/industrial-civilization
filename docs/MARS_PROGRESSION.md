# Mars Progression

The Mars route requires Galacticraft's Tier 2 rocket and normal survival systems. Establish a powered, oxygenated outpost and recover Desh, an existing Mars-only Galacticraft resource.

Desh ingot (`galacticraftplanets:item_basic_mars`, metadata 2) is the Martian scientific material. One ingot is required in the Analyzer recipe and another is consumed by a 6,250 EU analysis operation after returning to the industrial base. No new Martian ore or processing tree was added.

On Mars entry, the integration mod records `mars_visited` and whether `moon_visited` already existed. A missing Moon milestone is reported as a sequence violation. No Nether recipe supplies Desh or the Moon meteoric-iron requirement.
