# Human Space Ecology

Moon and Mars use the same human faction, reputation, trade, equipment, and salvage rules as Earth. Galacticraft's Evolved monsters and other monster identities are rejected; the pack does not reinterpret zombies, skeletons, creepers, spiders, or similar creatures as astronauts.

## Encounters and sites

- Space citizens are breathable human astronaut entities with faction-colored suits and normal IC Credit trade/reputation behavior.
- Space Pirates are the off-world robber analogue. They remain neutral unless attacked or the player carries strategic material from the current planet. Lunar pirates recognize raw Meteoric Iron, the Lunar Engineering Archive, and Lunar Quantum Components. Martian pirates recognize Desh, Martian Autonomy Archives, and Material Pattern Records.
- Territorial Space Militia use the Earth militia provocation and reputation rules. Off-world settlements may be Frontier/Survey/Riverside communities, militia sites, industrial cities, operational fabrication centers, criminal facilities, or abandoned pirate salvage sites.
- All issued weapons and worn armor are recoverable when the NPC is defeated. Additional sensible salvage—IC Credits, ammunition, oxygen hardware, Precision Frames, raw Meteoric Iron, or Desh—uses low independent drop chances.

## Equipment tiers

- Ordinary faction astronauts use their faction suit identity.
- Territorial Space Militia and fabrication-center engineers use QuantumSuit equipment.
- Space Pirates use ordinary astronaut gear 80% of the time and NanoSuit equipment 20% of the time.

## AI continuity

The magical Totem of Undying is removed from drops, Creative, recipes, and HEI. Its vanilla `Postmortal` advancement is overridden as `Postmortal Continuity`. A Robotic Manufacturing Cell can produce an AI Emergency Continuity Core from the durable AI Core, two Industrial Control Processors, and four Advanced Circuits. The consumable device prevents one lethal event, restores critical health, and is the sole success condition for the rewritten advancement.
